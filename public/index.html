4.1 CASE CLASSIFICATION

• History of fever within past 2 weeks AND
• At least two of the following: myalgia, headache, jaundice, conjunctival suffusion without purulent discharge, rash.

OR

• At least one: aseptic meningitis, GI symptoms, pulmonary complications, cardiac arrhythmias, acute renal failure WITH history of exposure to floodwaters/animals.

• Clinically compatible case with at least one of: involvement in an exposure event (triathlon, flooding) with known associated cases, OR presumptive lab findings without confirmatory MAT.

OR

• Suspect case in ongoing outbreak OR epidemiologic link + positive Rapid Test Kit.

• Suspect case that is laboratory confirmed.

Laboratory Confirmation:

• Isolation/typing from blood/urine culture of Leptospira.
• Positive serology (Microscopic Agglutination Test / MAT) using diagnostic titers.

8. KNOWLEDGE, ATTITUDE, & PRACTICE (DENGUE SURVEY)
Knowledge:
1. What causes dengue fever? (Ano ang sanhi ng dengue fever?)
2. What type of mosquito spreads dengue? (Anong uri ng lamok ang nagkakalat ng dengue?)
3. Can dengue cause severe complications like internal bleeding and organ failure? (Maaari bang magdulot ang dengue ng malubhang komplikasyon tulad ng internal bleeding at pagkasira ng laman-loob?)
4. Can a person get dengue more than once? (Maaari bang magkaroon ng dengue nang higit sa isang beses ang isang tao?)
5. Can dengue be fatal? (Maaari bang ikamatay ang dengue?)
Attitude:
6. Do you think dengue is a serious health threat in your community? (Sa tingin mo ba ay seryosong banta sa kalusugan ang dengue sa inyong komunidad?)
7. How worried are you about getting dengue? (Gaano ka nag-aalala na magkaroon ng dengue?)
8. Do you believe dengue prevention is a personal responsibility? (Naniniwala ka ba na ang pag-iwas sa dengue ay personal na responsibilidad?)
9. Do you believe regular cleanup drives are effective in reducing dengue cases? (Naniniwala ka ba na epektibo ang regular na cleanup drive sa pagbawas ng kaso ng dengue?)
10. If you had dengue symptoms, such as fever would you seek medical attention immediately? (Kung ikaw ay may sintomas ng dengue gaya ng lagnat, agad ka bang hihingi ng medikal na tulong?)
11. Do you think hospitals and health centers provide adequate care for dengue patients? (Sa tingin mo ba ay sapat ang serbisyong medikal ng mga ospital at health center para sa mga pasyenteng may dengue?)
12. If someone in your family had dengue, would you inform your barangay health workers or health center staff? (Kung may dengue ang isang miyembro ng pamilya mo, ipapaalam mo ba sa barangay health worker o staff ng health center?)
13. Would you allow your home to be inspected for mosquito breeding sites by health workers? (Papayag ka bang inspeksyunin ang iyong bahay para sa mga pinamumugaran ng lamok ng mga health worker?)
14. Would you be willing to get vaccinated against dengue if it is available? (Hahanda ka bang magpabakuna laban sa dengue kung ito ay available?)
15. If your home was identified as a mosquito breeding site, would you follow the recommendations given by health officials? (Kung natukoy na ang iyong bahay ay pinamumugaran ng lamok, susundin mo ba ang rekomendasyon ng mga health official?)
Practice:
16. How often do you check and remove stagnant water around your home? (Gaano kadalas mong chine-check at nililinis ang mga nakatigil na tubig sa paligid ng bahay?)
17. Do you use mosquito repellents at home? (Gumagamit ka ba ng mosquito repellent sa bahay?)
18. Do you use mosquito nets while sleeping? (Gumagamit ka ba ng kulambo habang natutulog?)
19. Do you wear protective clothing (long sleeves, pants) to avoid mosquito bites? (Nagsusuot ka ba ng proteksyong damit gaya ng long sleeves at pantalon para makaiwas sa kagat ng lamok?)
20. How often do you participate in barangay cleanup activities? (Gaano kadalas kang nakikilahok sa mga barangay cleanup activity?)
21. Where would you go first if you suspect someone in your family has dengue? (Saan ka unang pupunta kung pinaghihinalaan mong may dengue ang miyembro ng pamilya mo?)
22. Have you ever reported a suspected dengue case to your barangay or health officials? (Naireport mo na ba ang isang hinihinalang kaso ng dengue sa inyong barangay o health official?)
