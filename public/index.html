8. KNOWLEDGE, ATTITUDE, & PRACTICE (DENGUE SURVEY)
Knowledge:
1. What causes dengue fever? (Ano ang sanhi ng dengue fever?)
2. What type of mosquito spreads dengue? (Anong uri ng lamok?)
3. Can dengue cause severe internal bleeding and organ failure? (Maaari bang magdulot ng malubhang komplikasyon?)
4. Can a person get dengue more than once? (Maaari bang magkaroon ng dengue nang higit sa isang beses?)
5. Can dengue be fatal? (Maaari bang ikamatay ang dengue?)
Attitude:
6. Do you think dengue is a serious health threat in your community? (Seryosong banta sa kalusugan?)
7. How worried are you about getting dengue? (Gaano ka nag-aalala?)
8. Do you believe dengue prevention is a personal responsibility? (Personal na responsibilidad?)
9. Do you believe regular cleanup drives are effective? (Epektibo ang cleanup drive?)
10. If you had dengue symptoms, would you seek medical attention immediately? (Agad hihingi ng tulong?)
11. Do hospitals/centers provide adequate care for dengue patients?
12. If someone in family had dengue, would you inform barangay workers?
13. Would you allow your home to be inspected for mosquito breeding sites?
14. Would you be willing to get vaccinated against dengue if available?
15. If your home was identified as breeding site, would you follow recommendations?
Practice:
16. How often do you check and remove stagnant water around home?
17. Do you use mosquito repellents at home?
18. Do you use mosquito nets while sleeping?
19. Do you wear protective long sleeves/pants?
20. How often do you participate in barangay cleanup activities?
21. Where would you go first if you suspect dengue in your family?
22. Have you ever reported a suspected dengue case to officials?
